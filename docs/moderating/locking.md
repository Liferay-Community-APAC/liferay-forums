# Locking Topics

Lock a topic to end a discussion: everyone can still read it, but nobody can reply to it or edit its messages, moderators included.

1. Open the topic, or find it in the discussions list.

1. Open its **Options** menu (⋮) and click **Lock Topic**.

1. Confirm.

The topic shows the **Locked** badge and the message **This topic is locked. New replies and edits are not allowed.**

To allow replies again, click **Unlock Topic** in the same menu.
