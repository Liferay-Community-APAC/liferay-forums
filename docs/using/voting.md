# Voting

Votes show which topics and replies the community finds most useful.

- Click the **thumbs up** to upvote, or the **thumbs down** to downvote. The number between them is the total.
- Click the same button again to remove your vote, or the other button to change it.
- You can vote on topics and on each reply.
