# Subscriptions and Notifications

Follow a topic to be notified about every new reply.

## Following a Topic

- When you start a topic, **Subscribe to This message** is checked, so you follow your own topics.
- To follow another topic, open its **Options** menu (⋮) and click **Subscribe**. Click **Unsubscribe** to stop.

## What You're Notified About

| Event | Who Is Notified |
| --- | --- |
| A new reply | Everyone who follows the topic, except its author |
| A mention | The mentioned person, unless they already get the reply notification |
| An edited reply with new mentions | Only the newly mentioned people |

Notifications arrive in Liferay's notifications menu and by email. Emails include the start of the reply and a **View the discussion** link.

{: .note }
Email notifications only work once an administrator has set them up. See [Email Notifications](../administering/email-notifications.html).
