## Highlights

- **Discussions and questions.** Post a topic as an open discussion, or as a question that can be marked as solved.
- **Categories.** Organize topics into categories and one level of subcategories.
- **Mentions and subscriptions.** Mention members with `@`, and get web and email notifications for topics you follow.
- **Voting.** Upvote or downvote topics and replies.
- **Moderation.** Members report content. Moderators validate reports, lock topics, delete topics, and ban authors per site.
- **Search.** Find topics with facets for category, tag, author, and date.
- **Several sites.** Run separate forums, each with its own categories, topics, and bans.
- **13 languages.** Labels ship in English, Arabic, Catalan, Chinese, Dutch, Finnish, French, German, Hungarian, Japanese, Portuguese (Brazil), Spanish, and Swedish.
